# decoherence-classicality-paper
# Sean Hart, Spring 2026
# Title: 
Decoherence and the Emergence of Classicality: Appearance Without Ontological Resolution
# Keywords: 
Quantum Decoherence, Density Matrix, Open Quantum Systems, Pointer States, Entanglement, Measurement Problem, Quantum-to-Classical Transition
# Abstract: 
Decoherence has emerged as the leading framework for explaining the appearance of
classicality in quantum systems without modifying unitary quantum mechanics. This paper
evaluates whether decoherence alone provides a sufficient account of the quantum-to-classical
transition. The analysis covers environmental entanglement, the reduced density matrix
formalism, suppression of interference, and environment-induced superselection of pointer
states. Although experiments confirm decoherence and theory grounds it firmly, decoherence
produces an effective mixture rather than a genuine collapse, falling short of a complete account.
Three major interpretational frameworks (Many-Worlds, GRW, Bohmian) each incorporate
decoherence while treating it as insufficient. As section 5 will show, this convergence supports
the paper's central conclusion: a complete account of classicality requires addressing what it
means for a single outcome to be realized within a globally unitary theory.
